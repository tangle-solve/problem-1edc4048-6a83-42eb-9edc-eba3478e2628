### CHEM-000435: The fleeting Sb(III) hydride in disulfide hydroboration
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Measure the population and lifetime of the proposed hydridostibine between borane metathesis and Sb(I) regeneration. Obtain kinetic evidence discriminating a separately populated hydride from a concerted alternative.
- **Status:** The 2024 paper establishes Sb(I)/Sb(III) disulfide-hydroboration turnover and proposes the reaction sequence. Direct hydride-population measurements are a proposed mechanistic test of that account.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Tests the elementary event assigned to Sb–H formation and subsequent elimination.
- **Key works:** M. Huang, K. Li, Z. Zhang, and J. Zhou (2024), “Antimony Redox Catalysis: Hydroboration of Disulfides through Unique Sb(I)/Sb(III) Redox Cycling,” [DOI: 10.1021/jacs.4c05905](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c05905).
